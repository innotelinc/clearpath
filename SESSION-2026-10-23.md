## Decisions worth not re-litigating

- **SMTP submission, not the JMAP/HTTP API.** The app's mail layer is one function per
  provider behind a transport switch; SMTP keeps that shape and works with any submission
  service, not just Stalwart. No new abstraction was introduced.
- **Mailbox credentials, not Stalwart API keys** — the latter are not valid for SMTP AUTH.
- **`mustMatchSender` is on by default**, so `MAIL_FROM` must be the account's own address
  or an alias on it. A 535 means the mailbox; a sender rejection after a clean login means
  this setting.
- **No keys were copied into this environment.** Stripe, LOB, mail and AI all stay in the
  app's normal hidden-not-broken state here.

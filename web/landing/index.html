Clearpath — The credit-repair operating system behind Denovo Credit.

A multi-tenant workspace for credit-repair agencies, a white-labelled client portal, and a certified mailroom that turns approved disputes into printed, tracked packets. Server-rendered with Express, EJS and SQLite, and packaged as a container with a persistent volume.
